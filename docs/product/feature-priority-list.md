# StackHR Feature & Priority List

*Product roadmap and prioritisation framework — converted from
`StackHR_Feature_and_Priority_List.docx` for agent readability. Source of truth for wording
disputes remains the original docx.*

Priority model: **P0** = Critical · **P1** = High · **P2** = Later · **P3** = Future

---

## P0 — Critical Before / At Launch

**1. Payroll & Compliance Engine**
Monthly/term payroll processing, salary structures and pay grades, earnings and
deductions, PAYE calculation, pension calculation, statutory deductions, tax-rule
versioning, payroll approval workflow, payroll history, payroll audit trail, payslip
generation, CSV/export.
*Why: Payroll accuracy is StackHR's core trust proposition. A payroll error can immediately
damage customer confidence.*

**2. Employee Management**
Employee profiles, employee status, employment information, department/role, salary
information, bank/payment information, emergency contacts, document storage, employee
onboarding/offboarding workflows, employee leave management, employee self-service portal,
employee directory, organizational chart, employee search/filter, employee records history.
*Why: This is the foundation from which payroll and HR operations work.*

**3. Leave**
Leave types, leave balances, leave requests, approval workflows, leave history, leave
calendar.
*Why: Employers need a simple way to manage employee and staff availability and leave.*

**4. Expense & Spend Management**
Expense submission, expense approval, reimbursement, spend history, approval status.
*Why: This differentiates StackHR from a basic payroll product and supports the broader
people, payroll and business spending proposition.*

**5. User Roles & Permissions**
Admin, Manager, Employee, role-based permissions, organisation-level access control,
payroll approval permissions.
*Why: Employers need clear separation between HR, finance and general staff access.*

**6. Employee Self-Service / Dashboard**
View personal profile, document exchange, direct communication channels, view payslips,
request leave, view leave balance, submit expenses, track reimbursement, update permitted
personal information, view employment information.

**7. Security, Privacy & Auditability**
Role-based access control, audit logs, secure employee data, secure payroll data, data
encryption, session management, organisation isolation, privacy controls, data export,
account deletion/deactivation, security monitoring.
*Why: StackHR handles highly sensitive employee and financial information. Trust is a
product feature, not just a legal requirement.*

---

## P1 — High Priority After Launch
*Build these once the core payroll and HR workflows are stable and real customers are using
them.*

**Attendance** — Clock-in/clock-out timestamps, integration with payroll calculation,
automated overtime tracking, leave request syncing to avoid manual reconciliation.
*Why: Makes StackHR more relevant to the operating realities of businesses tracking hourly
staff.*

**Notifications & Communication** — Expense approval notifications, contract expiry
reminders, onboarding notifications, important HR announcements, email notifications,
in-app notifications.

**Advanced Reporting** — Department payroll, PAYE summary, pension summary, leave reports,
expense reports, headcount report, employee turnover, payroll cost trends, exportable
reports.

**Onboarding & Offboarding Automation** — Welcome emails, task assignment, offboarding
checklist, exit documentation, final payroll processing, access deactivation, recent HR
activity feed, payroll cost tracking, staff changes, important alerts.

---

## P2 — Important Differentiators
*Build these once the core product has demonstrated product-market fit.*

- **Advanced Attendance** — clock-in/out, dashboard, late arrivals, absence tracking,
  reports, biometric integration where practical.
- **Performance Management** — reviews, review cycles, objectives, manager feedback,
  self-assessment, history, evaluation templates.
- **Recruitment** — job openings, applicant tracking, candidate profiles, interview
  scheduling, hiring pipeline, offer management, candidate-to-employee conversion.
- **Document Management** — employee documents, contracts, certificates, ID documents,
  policy acknowledgements, expiry tracking, access permissions.
- **HR Analytics** — headcount trends, payroll trends, turnover, absenteeism, leave
  utilisation, department analysis, employee cost analysis, workforce trends.

---

## P3 — Future / Expansion
*Should not distract the team during initial launch.*

- **Benefits Management** — health insurance, allowances, staff benefits, eligibility,
  deductions.
- **Advanced Financial Management** — budgets, department budgets, spend limits, financial
  forecasting, advanced expense analytics.
- **Integrations** — accounting software, banking platforms, biometric attendance systems,
  Google Workspace/Microsoft 365.
- **AI Features** *(Experimental)* — AI HR assistant, payroll anomaly detection, HR policy
  assistant, automated HR reports, employee insights, payroll explanations, document
  extraction, HR workflow recommendations.

---

## The Product Priority Rule
Every proposed feature should pass these questions:
1. Does it solve a real problem for our target customers?
2. Does it increase payroll/HR accuracy or reduce administrative work?
3. Will customers actually use it frequently?
4. Does it improve retention or willingness to pay?

## Immediate Product Focus
Payroll reliability → Compliance accuracy → Employee management → Leave → Spend →
Permissions/security → Reporting → Employee self-service.

The objective is not to have the most features. The objective is to make an admin trust the
product with their company's payroll on day one.
